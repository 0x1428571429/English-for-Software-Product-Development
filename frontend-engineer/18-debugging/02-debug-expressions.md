### "It was working yesterday"
刚才还好好的。

还可以说：
- It worked before
- Nothing changed (but something did)
- What happened?
- It broke overnight

### "Let me check the git log"
看谁改了什么。

还可以说：
- Let me see what changed
- Check the latest commits
- Someone merged something
- Let's see the blame

### "Let me comment out the new code"
二分法排查。

还可以说：
- Let me isolate the problem
- Let me revert the change and test
- Let me narrow it down
- Let me test one thing at a time

### "AI confirmed our diagnosis"
AI 和你的判断一致。

还可以说：
- AI suggested the same thing
- AI agrees with our finding
- I pasted the error into AI
- AI pointed to the same issue
