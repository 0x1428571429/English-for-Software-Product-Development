### "Let me walk you through it step by step"

教用户操作。

还可以说：
- Click here first
- Then fill this out
- After that, click submit
- Let me show you on my screen

### "Can you try clearing your cache?"

经典的缓存问题。

还可以说：
- Try a hard refresh
- Clear your browser cache
- Use incognito mode
- Ctrl+Shift+Delete
