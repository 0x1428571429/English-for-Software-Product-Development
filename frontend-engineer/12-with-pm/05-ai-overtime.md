### "What if AI can speed it up?"

PM 觉得 AI 是万能药。

还可以说：
- Can AI help with this?
- Let's throw AI at it
- AI can probably do it faster
- Why can't AI just write this?

回应：
- AI can help but someone needs to review
- AI won't solve the architecture problem
- The bottleneck isn't code, it's requirements
- AI still needs a human to validate
