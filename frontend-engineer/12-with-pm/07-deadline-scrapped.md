### "PM wants an exact date"
PM 要精确日期。
还可以说：
- I can't give you an exact date
- I'd estimate 2-3 days but no promises
- There's too many unknowns
- I'll know more after I start

### "So the work I did last week is being scrapped?"
需求作废白做了。
还可以说：
- We're dropping this feature?
- All that work for nothing?
- Can we reuse any of it?
- This is why I don't start early

### "The PM keeps asking for status every 2 hours"
PM 催太频繁。
还可以说：
- I'll update you at EOD
- Asking every hour doesn't make it faster
- I'll ping you when there's progress
- Trust me to do my work
