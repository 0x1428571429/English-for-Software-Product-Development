### "Let's do a spike"

先做个原型试试。

还可以说：
- Let's prototype it first
- Let's validate the approach
- One day spike
- Let's prove it works before committing

### "I'll prototype it with AI"

用 AI 做原型。

还可以说：
- Let me ask AI to generate a demo
- AI can help build the POC
- I'll use AI for the initial version
- Let AI generate the boilerplate

### "It works in the spike but will it scale?"

原型没问题不代表生产环境能用。

还可以说：
- The spike doesn't cover edge cases
- We need to consider production load
- Prototype is not a production solution
- It's easy to make it work once
