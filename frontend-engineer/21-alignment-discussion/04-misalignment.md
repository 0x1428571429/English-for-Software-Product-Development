### "I thought we agreed on Wednesday. That's not what I remember."

信息不对称，两边的理解不一样。

还可以说：
- I remember it differently
- That's not what we discussed last time
- When did that change?
- I wasn't aware of that decision

### "No one told me about this change."

信息没传达到。

还可以说：
- I wasn't informed
- I missed that memo
- Was this communicated?
- When did this get decided?
