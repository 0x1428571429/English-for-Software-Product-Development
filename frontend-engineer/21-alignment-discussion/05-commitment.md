### "You shouldn't have committed to that date without checking the team."

不应该对外承诺了再跟团队说。

还可以说：
- Don't promise dates without asking us
- Check with the team first
- We need team consensus before promises
- No more external commitments

### "Let's get on the same page"

对齐信息。

还可以说：
- Let's align on this
- We need to clarify expectations
- Let's make sure we all agree
- Let's sync up
