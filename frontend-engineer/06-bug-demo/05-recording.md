### "Let me send you the screenshot"
录屏/截图发给别人。
还可以说：
- Let me record it and send it over
- I'll take a video of the bug
- Here's a screenshot of what I'm seeing
- Let me capture it before it goes away

### "This is a third-party issue, we can't fix it"
bug 是第三方服务的问题。
还可以说：
- It's a third-party API issue
- We're dependent on an external service
- We've reported it to the vendor
- There's nothing we can do on our end
