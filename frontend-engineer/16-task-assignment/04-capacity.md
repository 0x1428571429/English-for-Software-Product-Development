### "I'm at capacity. I already have three tickets."

活太多，接不了新的。

还可以说：
- My plate is full
- I'm overcommitted
- I can't take anything else
- I need to finish what I have first

### "Can you pair with X on this?"

安排老人带新人。

还可以说：
- Can you mentor X on this?
- X needs some guidance here
- Can you be the point person?
- Help X get started
