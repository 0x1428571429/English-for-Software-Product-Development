### "It was working earlier"
演示翻车现场。最经典的 demo 翻车台词。

Also say：
- It worked fine yesterday
- Something must have changed
- Let me try again
- Give me a sec to debug this

### "Is that the final design?"
效果跟预期不一样。

Also say：
- That looks different from what I expected
- The spacing is off
- The colors don't match the brand
- I thought we agreed on X

### "I don't have the answer off the top of my head"
答不上来。

Also say：
- Good question. I'll need to check.
- I'm not sure. Let me get back to you.
- That's outside my scope. Let me find out.
- I'll follow up after the demo.

### "It's mocked"
大部分功能还没做。

Also say：
- The data is mocked
- The real integration isn't done yet
- This is just the UI placeholder
- The backend isn't ready yet

### AI推荐的方案不行

- AI suggested it but it wasn't compatible
- The AI solution didn't fit our architecture
- I tried AI's approach but it caused other issues
