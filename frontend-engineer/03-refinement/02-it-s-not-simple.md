## "Simple? The search backend doesn't support that yet."

PM 说"很简单"，但实际很复杂。

Also say：
- That's not as simple as it sounds
- There's more to it than you think
- It looks simple on the surface
- That requires a lot of backend work

### Related conversation

> B: Sure. Users want to search by product name, category, and price range. Simple.
> C: Simple? The search backend doesn't support price range yet.
> B: Can we add it?
> C: Everything is possible. But it's not simple.

Everything is possible — 什么都能做，但...

- Everything is possible. Just not in this sprint. — 什么都能做，但不是这个 sprint
- Everything is possible with enough time. — 时间够都能做
- It's possible, but it's a lot of work. — 能做，但工作量很大

### PM 说"应该很快"

> B: OK then just name and category for now. Should be quick.

- Should be quick — 应该很快
- That shouldn't take long — 应该不用多久
- It's just a small change — 只是个小改动

### When to use

- PM 低估了开发工作量时
- 需要给 PM 解释复杂度时
- PM 觉得"加个字段"很简单时
