## "That's a completely different feature. That's autocomplete, not search."

PM 把不同功能混为一谈，要纠正。

还可以说：
- That's two different features
- That's not what we discussed
- That's a separate concern
- Don't conflate X and Y

### 关联对话

> B: Also, the search should show results in real-time as the user types.
> E: That's a completely different feature. That's autocomplete, not search.
> B: It's the same thing.
> E: No, it's not. Autocomplete is way more complex.

- No, it's not — 不，不是一回事
- That's way more complex — 复杂多了
- We need a different endpoint for that
- That requires a separate implementation

### PM 说"AI能不能加速"

> B: Can AI help speed this up?
> E: AI can't build your backend for you.

- AI can't build your backend for you — AI 不能帮你建后端
- AI won't solve this — AI 解决不了这个
- This is an infrastructure problem, not an AI problem
- AI can help with some parts, but not the core logic

### 什么时候说

- PM 把不同功能混在一起说时
- PM 用 AI 作为万能加速器时
- 需要澄清功能边界时
