## "Wait, earlier you said price range too. Which one is it?"

PM 前后矛盾，需求变来变去。

还可以说：
- Earlier you said X, now you're saying Y
- Which one is it?
- I'm confused — the requirement keeps changing
- You're contradicting yourself

### 关联对话

> D: Wait, earlier you said price range too. Which one is it?
> B: Well, I'd like all three. But if it's too hard, just do two.
> C: That's not how it works. We need a clear spec.

- That's not how it works — 不是这样做的
- We need a clear spec — 我们需要清晰的需求
- We can't build something that keeps changing
- Let's get this written down first

### PM 说"大概"

> B: I said "probably."
> C: So you committed without an estimate. Great.

- So you committed without asking us — 你没问我们就承诺了
- That's a sales promise, not a dev estimate — 这是销售的承诺，不是开发的估算
- Please check with us before promising — 请先跟我们确认再承诺
- We need to stop doing this — 别再这样了

### 谁的需求听谁的

> B: The stakeholders are expecting this by end of sprint.

- Did you tell them it's possible before checking with us?
- We can't be held to external promises we didn't make
- Next time, include us in the stakeholder meeting

### 什么时候说

- PM 需求前后不一致时
- PM 没确认就对外承诺时
- 需求变来变去，开发受不了时
