### "I see your point, but here's why I think differently"

被 challenge 时的反驳。

还可以说：
- I understand what you're saying, but...
- That's a fair point, however...
- I respectfully disagree because...
- Let me explain why I see it differently

### 折中方案

> How about we meet in the middle?

还可以说：
- Let's compromise
- Can we find a middle ground?
- How about we do half now, half later?
- What if we do a simpler version first?

### 给需求排优先级

> Is this a must-have or a nice-to-have?

还可以说：
- What's the priority on this?
- Is this blocking or can it wait?
- Do we need this for launch?
- Can we defer this?
