### "This isn't feasible with our current setup"

技术上做不了。

还可以说：
- Technically this isn't possible
- We don't have the infrastructure for this
- It would require a complete rewrite
- The backend doesn't support it

### 关联对话

> C: Simple? The search backend doesn't support price range yet.
> B: Can we add it?
> C: Everything is possible. But it's not simple.

Everything is possible — 什么都能做，只是代价问题：

- Everything is possible with enough time and money
- It's possible but it's a huge effort
- Technically feasible but not practical
- We could do it, but it would break other things
