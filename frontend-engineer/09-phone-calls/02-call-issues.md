### "Can you hear me?"
电话开场确认能不能听到。

还可以说：
- Are you there?
- Can you hear me OK?
- You're breaking up
- I can barely hear you

### "You cut out"
信号断了/卡顿。

还可以说：
- You cut out for a second
- Your audio is choppy
- You froze
- You're glitching

### "Let me turn off the video"
关视频改善网络。

还可以说：
- Video is killing my bandwidth
- Let me go audio only
- Can we turn off cameras?

### "Bear with me"
找东西/准备中。

还可以说：
- Bear with me, let me find it
- One sec, I'm looking for it
- Give me a moment
- Let me pull that up

### "What did I miss?"
中途加入。

还可以说：
- Sorry I'm late. What did I miss?
- Can you catch me up?
- I just joined. What's the context?
- Can you summarize what I missed?

### 你没开麦
- You're on mute
- I think you're muted
- We can't hear you

### 结束通话
- That's all from my side
- Anything else?
- Talk to you Thursday
- Thanks. Bye.
