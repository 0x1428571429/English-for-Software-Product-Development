### "I can't understand their accent"
听不懂对方的口音。
还可以说：
- Sorry, I'm having trouble understanding
- Could you repeat that more slowly?
- Can you type that in the chat?
- I'm not familiar with that accent

### "Your VPN is causing the issue"
VPN 断了连不上会议。
还可以说：
- My VPN disconnected
- I need to reconnect to the VPN
- Can you hear me now?
- The connection keeps dropping

### "The AI translation was wrong"
AI 实时翻译翻错了。
还可以说：
- The AI captions were completely wrong
- The auto-translate didn't work
- I was reading the captions and they made no sense
- Let me turn off the AI subtitles
