## "Can AI help generate some of the test data?"

让 AI 帮忙生成测试数据。

还可以说：
- Let AI generate the mock data
- Can AI write some test cases?
- Ask AI to create test scenarios

### 关联对话

> C: I can ask AI to generate mock data. But you'll need to verify it.
> B: Fine by me.

- AI can generate it but you need to verify — AI 能生成但你要验证
- Don't trust AI-generated test data blindly
- AI can speed things up but QA still needs to review

### 让 AI 写测试

- AI wrote the unit tests, I just reviewed them
- AI generated the test cases
- I had AI cover the edge cases

### AI 测试的问题

- AI doesn't know the business logic — AI 不了解业务
- The AI tests passed but missed the real issue
- AI generated test data but it was invalid

### 什么时候说

- 准备测试数据太耗时
- 让 AI 帮忙写测试
- 提醒 AI 生成的数据要验证
