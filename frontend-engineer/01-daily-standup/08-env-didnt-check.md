## "Staging was down yesterday. Couldn't test anything."

环境挂了，一整天没法干活。

还可以说：
- Dev environment was broken all day
- The staging server was down
- I couldn't test anything because the env was down

### 没确认环境恢复了

> I: I think so? I didn't check today.

早上不确认环境：
- I didn't check yet — 还没看
- I'll check after this — 会后确认一下
- Let me verify and let you know — 我确认一下告诉你

### 环境问题的常用表达

- Is the environment back up? — 环境恢复了？
- The staging server is down — staging 挂了
- I'm getting a 502 error — 502 报错
- The database connection is failing — 数据库连不上

### 什么时候说

- 环境崩了没法干活时
- 被问到环境状态但不确定时
- 需要让别人确认环境时
