## "The PM asked me to add another field. I already have three tasks on my plate."

手里一堆活，PM 又加需求，但不敢拒绝。

还可以说：
- I have too much on my plate
- I'm already overloaded
- I don't have bandwidth for this
- I can't take on anything else right now

### 不敢拒绝

> H: I said "I'll see what I can do."
> A: So you didn't say no.
> H: I don't want to push back.

"I'll see what I can do" — 听着像"好的"，其实心里想的是"别加了"。但对方只会以为你接了这个活。

还可以说：
- Let me check my workload and get back to you — 我看下我的工作量再回复你
- I need to prioritize — can we discuss this? — 我需要排优先级
- I'm at capacity right now — 我现在已经满负荷了

### 帮下属挡需求

> A: Focus on your current tasks. I'll talk to the PM.

- Focus on what you're on — 做你现在的事
- I'll handle the PM — 我跟 PM 说
- Let me push back on that for you — 我帮你去回绝
- Don't worry about it, I'll take care of it — 别管了，我来处理

### 什么时候说

- 手里活太多，PM 又加需求时
- 不敢直接拒绝，但需要表态时
- 作为负责人帮团队成员挡需求时
