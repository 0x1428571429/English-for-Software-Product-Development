## "Let's move."

站会结束语。但总是有人喊完又喊住，走不了。

还可以说：
- Alright, let's wrap up.
- Thanks everyone. Let's get to work.
- Cool. Moving on.

### 分配任务收尾

> A: Alex — your PR needs review. Mark — I want to see progress today. Tom — sort out the merge conflict.

站会结束前点名：
- X — do this.
- X — I want to see progress today.
- X — sort out this issue.
- X — keep me posted.

### "Oh wait, one more thing"

> D: Oh wait, one more thing. I need access to the staging DB.

散会前突然想起一件事：
- Oh wait, one more thing — 等一下，还有一件事
- Before we wrap up... — 结束之前...
- Actually, I also wanted to mention... — 还有一件事想说
- Real quick — 很快说一下

### 不知道找谁

> D: I'm not sure. I asked in the DevOps channel yesterday but no one replied.

不知道谁负责：
- I'm not sure who handles that — 我不知道谁负责
- I asked in the channel but no one responded — 在频道问了没人回
- Who's the right person for this? — 这个该找谁？

还可以说：
- Can you point me to the right person? — 你能告诉我该找谁吗？
- Who should I talk to about this? — 这个该跟谁说？

### "Real quick" — 从来没快过

> E: Actually, real quick — can we also talk about the new auth flow? I have some concerns.
> A: That's not a standup topic. Let's take it offline.
> E: It'll be quick.
> A: It always is. Slack me the details.

嘴上说很快，实际要聊很久：

主持人的应对：
- That's not a standup topic — 这不是站会该聊的
- Let's take it offline — 私下聊
- Slack me the details — Slack 上说
- We can set up a separate meeting for that — 另开会聊

还可以说：
- Not now. Let's discuss it after. — 现在不说，会后聊
- That deserves its own conversation — 这个值得单独聊
- Put it on the backlog for next refinement — 放到下次需求评审

### 沉默

> (silence)

问 "Anyone else?" 没人回应：
- Anyone else? — 还有吗？
- Nothing else? — 没别的了？
- OK, moving on. — 好的，继续。

### 驱散大家

> A: Alright. Go. Get back to work.

赶人：
- Go get back to work — 回去干活
- That's it. See you tomorrow. — 就这样，明天见
- Let's go build stuff — 去做事了

### 什么时候说

- 站会结束时
- 有人散会前突然插话时
- 有人想展开聊不该站会聊的话题时
- 分配完任务收尾时
- 问大家还有没有话，没人回应时
