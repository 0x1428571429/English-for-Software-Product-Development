## "I don't really know what's going on right now."

请假回来，完全不知道项目进度。

还可以说：
- I'm still catching up. Not sure what's happening.
- Let me get up to speed first.
- I need to check what I missed.

### 没人交接

> A: Did anyone fill her in?
> (silence)

没人主动交代：
- No one filled me in — 没人告诉我
- I don't have context on this — 我没有上下文
- Can someone give me a quick summary? — 谁能给我说一下？

### 忘了昨天做了什么

> G: Uh... I think I was on the search filter? Let me check my notes.
> A: You don't remember?

- Let me check my notes — 我看下笔记
- Give me a sec to look it up — 等我查一下
- I think I was on X? — 应该是 X 吧
- It's been a long week — 这周太长了

### 互相推卸

> C: I told you to sync before you started.
> G: You didn't tell me anything.
> C: I sent you a message.
> G: I didn't see it.

没看到消息：
- I didn't see your message — 我没看到
- Sorry, I must have missed it — 抱歉，应该是漏了
- Can you send it again? — 能再发一次吗？

### 什么时候说

- 请假回来什么都不知道时
- 忘了昨天做了什么时
- 跟同事有沟通问题，互相甩锅时
