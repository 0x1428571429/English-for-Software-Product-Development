## "Dashboard is done. PR was up yesterday."

做完了，PR 提了。但没人 review，卡在那没人管。

还可以说：
- Done on my end. Waiting for review.
- PR's been sitting there since yesterday.
- Just need someone to sign off.

### 被反问

> A: Did anyone review it?
> C: No. Still waiting.

没人 review 时：
- Still waiting — 还在等
- No one's looked at it yet — 还没人看
- I asked in the channel but no one replied — 发消息了没人回

### 安排别人 review

> A: Sarah, can you take a look?

安排 review：
- Can you review Alex's PR?
- Can you take a look when you're free?
- Who can review this?

### CI 挂了

PR 卡在 CI 上：
- CI is failing — not sure if it's my change
- There's a flaky test
- The build is red

### 什么时候说

- 做完任务等 review 时
- PR 没人看，想催一下时
