### 有人迟到

> "Sorry I'm late. What did I miss?"

还可以说：
- Just joined. Can someone catch me up?
- Late again, sorry. Anything for me?
- Did I miss anything important?
- Can you fill me in after?

### 时区问题/没睡醒

还可以说：
- It's 6 AM here, bear with me
- Early morning for me, I might be slow
- I just woke up, give me a sec
- Different timezone, I'm half asleep

### "Works on my machine"

别人跑不起来但本地可以。

还可以说：
- It works on my machine, not sure why
- Works locally but not on staging
- Can you check your environment?
- It must be a config difference

### AI写了站会更新但不对

还可以说：
- Sorry, AI wrote that update. Let me correct it.
- The AI-generated status is wrong. Here's the real update.
- I used AI to write my standup update but it's inaccurate.
- Let me clarify what I actually did yesterday.
