## "Yeah, still working on it. Three days. But it's almost done."

三天了还没做完，还要说"快好了"，但其实还差得远。

还可以说：
- It's taking way longer than expected
- I've been going in circles on this
- This one is killing me

### 被拆穿

> A: You said that yesterday too.
> E: I know. I'm just running into some issues.

被拆穿之后怎么办：
- I know, I know. I'm on it. — 我知道，我在弄
- I keep hitting new problems. — 一直遇到新问题
- Every time I fix one thing, something breaks. — 修一个炸一个

### AI 帮倒忙

> E: AI generated some code but honestly it created more problems than it solved.

说 AI 不行：
- AI made it worse — AI 搞得更糟了
- The AI code was full of bugs — AI 的代码一堆 bug
- I spent more time fixing AI's code than writing it myself — 修 AI 的代码比自己写还久
- AI suggested something completely wrong — AI 给的东西完全不对

### 砍需求

> A: Let's sync after. Might need to cut scope.

### 什么时候说

- 任务做了很久还没做完时
- 被 PM 或 tech lead 追问时
- AI 帮倒忙时
