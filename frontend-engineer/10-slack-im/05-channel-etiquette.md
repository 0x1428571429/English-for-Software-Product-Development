### "Wrong channel, sorry"

发错了频道。

还可以说：
- Wrong channel
- Meant to send this to #random
- Sorry, wrong thread

### "Jumping in here"

被拉进一个 thread。

还可以说：
- Adding some context here
- I worked on this feature, let me chime in
- Just saw this thread

### "Did you see my message?"

对方在线但不回。

还可以说：
- Hey, bumping this
- Not sure if you saw my message
- I know you're busy but just checking

### Ghosted

看到消息故意不回。

还可以说：
- I saw you read it but no reply
- Left me on read
- It's OK if you don't know, just say so

### 什么时候说

- 不小心发错频道时
- 被拉到 thread 里时
- 对方看到消息但不回时
