- I ran it through AI and it pointed to X
- AI says it's a cache issue
- AI suggested reinstalling node_modules
- Let me ask AI real quick

### "Let me ask AI"

先问 AI 再问人。

还可以说：
- Let me check with AI first
- AI might know this one
- One sec, let me ask AI

### AI 的回答太明显

发了一段 AI 生成的回复，味道太明显。

还可以说：
- (someone replies) This sounds like AI wrote this
- Did you just ask AI to respond?
- I can tell this is AI-generated

### 什么时候说

- Slack 上分享 AI 的分析结果
- 先问 AI 再打扰别人
- AI 回复味道太明显被同事发现
