### "This sounds like AI wrote this"
AI 回复味道太明显。
还可以说：
- Did you just ask AI to respond?
- This sounds AI-generated
- Can you respond in your own words?
- I can tell this is from AI

### "Can you type that? I can't listen right now"
语音消息不方便听。
还可以说：
- Can you send it as text?
- I'm in a meeting, can't listen
- Voice messages are hard to search
- Just paste it here please

### "Wrong channel, sorry"
发错了。
还可以说：
- Meant to send that to #random
- Sorry, wrong thread
- Can a mod delete that?
- Ignore that, wrong place
