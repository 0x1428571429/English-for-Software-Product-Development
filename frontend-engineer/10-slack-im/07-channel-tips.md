### "Hey @x, do you know about this?"
在 channel 里@人。
还可以说：
- @x can you help with this?
- Pinging @x for visibility
- @x any thoughts on this?
- Tagging @x who worked on this before

### "Jumping in here"
被拉进一个 thread。
还可以说：
- Adding some context
- I worked on this feature, let me chime in
- Just saw this thread — my two cents
- Let me clarify since I was involved
