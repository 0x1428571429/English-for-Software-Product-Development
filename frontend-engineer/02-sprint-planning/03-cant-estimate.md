## "I don't know how to estimate it."

新人/不熟的人不知道怎么估。

还可以说：
- I've never worked on this before
- I don't have enough context to estimate
- I need to look into it first
- Hard to say without more information

### 关联对话

> D: I've never worked on checkout before. I don't know how to estimate it.
> C: I can help you break it down after this.

老人/tech lead 帮忙：
- I can help you break it down — 我帮你拆一下
- Let's pair on the estimate — 我们一起估
- Let me walk you through what's involved — 我带你过一遍
- I'll help you figure it out — 我帮你搞定

### 需求模糊没法估

> E: We're waiting on the payment provider's API doc. Hard to estimate without it.
> A: Can you give a rough range?
> E: Anywhere from 3 to 8 days. Depends on what their API looks like.

给一个范围：
- Anywhere from 3 to 8 days
- Rough estimate is 5 days
- Ballpark figure — 大概数字
- It's hard to say until we have the doc

❌ I can't estimate — 太绝对，至少给个范围

### 常见错误

❌ This task very difficult to estimate — 缺少 "is"，应该说 "This task is very difficult to estimate"

### 什么时候说

- 需求不清晰时
- 没做过类似功能时
- 依赖外部因素时（第三方 API、文档等）
