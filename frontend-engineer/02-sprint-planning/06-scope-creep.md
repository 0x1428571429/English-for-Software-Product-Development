## "That's a whole new feature. Not in scope."

PM 临场加需求，要挡住。

还可以说：
- That's not in scope
- That's a separate ticket
- That's scope creep
- We didn't plan for that

### 关联对话

> B: Oh, also — I forgot to mention. We need to add SSO login.
> C: That's a whole new feature. Not in scope.
> B: But they asked for it.
> C: That's a separate ticket. Let's not scope creep here.

PM 说"客户要的"时：
- They asked for it — 客户要的
- I forgot to mention — 我忘了说
- It's a small thing — 很小的需求（其实不小）

挡住的话术：
- That's a separate ticket — 这是另一个 ticket
- Let's not scope creep — 别 scope creep
- We can add it to the next sprint
- Put it in the backlog

### 强行让估

> B: Can we at least estimate it?
> C: Fine. 3 days minimum. But something has to come out if we add this.

- Can we at least estimate it? — 至少估一下？
- Fine. X days minimum. — 好吧，至少 X 天
- Something has to come out — 加进来就得拿掉别的

### 什么时候说

- PM 在 planning 上临时加需求时
- 想保护 sprint 范围时
- 被迫接受新需求但要求砍别的
