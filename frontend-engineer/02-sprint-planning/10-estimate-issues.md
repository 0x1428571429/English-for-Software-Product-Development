### "I'll give it my best shot, but no promises"
被迫接受不合理估点。

还可以说：
- I'll try but I can't guarantee it
- I'll do what I can
- I'm not confident about this timeline
- Don't hold me to this date

### "I think you're underestimating this"
被人低估了工作量。

还可以说：
- This is more complex than it looks
- I think you're being optimistic
- Have you considered the edge cases?
- I'd double that estimate

### "We shouldn't cut corners on testing"
团队策略问题。

还可以说：
- Skipping testing will bite us later
- We need proper QA
- Quality shouldn't be sacrificed for speed
- Technical debt adds up

### 把 bug fix 当新故事估点

还可以说：
- This is a bug, not a new feature
- We should fix it without eating into new feature time
- Bug fixes should be separate from feature work
- This was working before, it's a regression

### AI 估点和人估的不一样

还可以说：
- AI estimated 2 days but I think it's 5
- AI doesn't know our codebase
- AI's estimate is too optimistic
- Let's go with the human estimate
