## "Everything is priority."

PM 说所有需求都重要，但 sprint 容量有限。

还可以说：
- It's all high priority
- We need all of it
- I can't choose — they're all important

### 关联对话

> A: PM, what's your priority?
> B: Everything.
> C: Then we can't commit to any of it.

"都是优先级"就是没优先级：
- Then we can't commit to any of it — 那我们一个都不能承诺
- If everything is priority, nothing is — 都是优先级就是没有优先级
- You need to pick one
- We can't deliver all of it

### 砍需求

> A: PM, pick one.
> B: Fine. Dashboard in. Migration out.

让 PM 做选择：
- Pick one
- Choose
- What's the most important?
- If we can only do one, which one?

### 风险 vs 价值

> C: But migration has the highest risk. If we don't do it now, it'll bite us later.

- It'll bite us later — 以后会反噬
- It's technical debt
- The risk is too high to ignore
- We need to address this now

### 什么时候说

- PM 不肯取舍时
- sprint 容量不够需要砍需求时
- 技术债和高价值 feature 冲突时
