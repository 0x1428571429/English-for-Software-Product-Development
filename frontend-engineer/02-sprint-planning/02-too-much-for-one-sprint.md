## "That's a lot for one sprint."

PM 想塞太多事，开发说做不完。

Also say：
- This is way too much for one sprint
- We can't fit all of this in
- This is unrealistic for two weeks
- There's no way we can deliver all of this

### Common mistakes

❌ This sprint have too many tasks — 语法错，have 应该是 has

❌ We can't finish so many works — works 不可数，应该说 so much work

### Related conversation

> B: Well, we need all of them by end of next month.
> C: Doesn't mean we can do them all in one sprint.

PM 说 deadline 时的回应：
- Doesn't mean we can do it — 不代表我们做得完
- That's not our problem — 那不是我们的事（比较直接）
- We can only commit to what fits — 我们只承诺能做完的
- The timeline doesn't change the estimate — 时间线不影响估点

### 容量不够

> A: We have 15 days of capacity. Something has to go.

- Something has to go — 必须砍掉一些
- We don't have enough capacity
- The sprint is already full
- We're over capacity

### 被迫接受

> A: PM, pick one.

让 PM 做选择：
- What's your priority?
- Pick one.
- Which one do you want to drop?
- You need to choose.

### When to use

- PM 给太多需求时
- sprint 容量不够时
- 需要让 PM 做优先级选择时
