## "Who wants to take this?"

没人想接这个任务。

还可以说：
- Anyone up for this?
- Someone needs to pick this up
- Who's available for this?

### 沉默

> A: Migration. Who wants to take this?
> (silence)
> A: Anyone?

没人回应时的追问：
- Anyone? — 有人吗
- Hello? — 喂（半开玩笑）
- Someone has to do it — 总得有人做
- I'll assign it if no one volunteers — 没人自愿我就指派人

### 不情愿地接了

> E: I'll do it but I'm not confident on the timeline.

接了但没把握：
- I'll do it but I'm not confident — 我来做但没把握
- I can take it but I need help
- I'll give it a shot
- I'll try but no promises

### AI 估算 vs 人估算

> E: AI estimated it at 2 days but I think it's more like 5.
> C: AI doesn't know our codebase.

- AI estimated it at X but I think it's Y — AI 估了 X 但我觉得是 Y
- AI doesn't know our codebase — AI 不了解我们的代码
- AI's estimate is too optimistic — AI 估得太乐观
- Let's go with the human estimate — 用人估的

### 什么时候说

- 有个难啃的任务没人想接
- 冷场时打破沉默
- AI 估算和人的估算不一致时
