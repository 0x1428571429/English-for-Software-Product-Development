### "I asked AI but I still don't understand"

AI 解决了但不知道为什么。

还可以说：
- AI gave me the answer but I don't get it
- It worked but I'm not sure why
- AI fixed it — I just trusted it
- I need a human explanation

### "Should I ask AI or wait for you?"

不确定该先问 AI 还是直接找人。

还可以说：
- Can AI handle this or do I need you?
- Let me try AI first
- I'll ask AI and if it's wrong I'll ping you
- Do you want me to ask AI first?
