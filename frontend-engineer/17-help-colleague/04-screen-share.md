### "Can you share your screen?"

远程帮同事的第一步。

还可以说：
- Let me see what you're seeing
- Share your screen and show me
- Can you walk me through it?
- Let me take a look

### "I tried AI already but it wasn't helpful"

同事先试了 AI 再来找你。

还可以说：
- AI suggested something but it was wrong
- AI wasn't helpful on this one
- I asked AI first but it doesn't know our codebase
- AI gave me the wrong direction
