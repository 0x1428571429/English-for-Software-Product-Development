### "Do you have a sec?"
求助开场。

还可以说：
- Got a minute?
- Are you free?
- Can I ask you something?
- I'm stuck on something

### "Let me take a look"
愿意帮忙。

还可以说：
- Let me see
- Show me what's happening
- Can you share your screen?
- Let's debug it together

### "I tried asking AI"
先试过了 AI 再来找人。

还可以说：
- AI suggested something but it wasn't right
- I already asked AI
- AI wasn't helpful
- AI gave me the wrong approach

### "I owe you one"
欠人情。

还可以说：
- Thanks, I appreciate it
- You saved me
- Thanks for your help
- Let me know if I can return the favor

### "That's what I'm here for"
同事间互帮。

还可以说：
- No problem
- Happy to help
- Anytime
- That's what teammates are for
